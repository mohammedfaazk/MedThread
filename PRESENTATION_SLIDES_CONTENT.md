## PRESENTATION TIPS

### **Delivery Guidelines**:
1. **Start Strong**: Hook with the disease prediction story
2. **Show, Don't Tell**: Live demo is crucial
3. **Tell Stories**: Use patient/doctor testimonials
4. **Be Confident**: You're solving a real problem
5. **Handle Questions**: Prepare for technical deep-dives

### **Time Allocation** (20-minute presentation):
- Problem: 2 minutes
- Solution: 3 minutes
- Technology: 4 minutes
- Live Demo: 5 minutes
- Business Model: 2 minutes
- Ask & Close: 2 minutes
- Q&A: 2 minutes

### **Visual Design**:
- Use consistent color scheme (medical teal, blue, orange)
- Include icons and illustrations
- Keep text minimal (bullet points)
- Use high-quality screenshots
- Add animations for key points

### **Backup Plans**:
- Have video recording of demo (if live fails)
- Print handouts with key metrics
- Prepare for technical questions
- Have financial projections ready
- Know your competitors inside-out
