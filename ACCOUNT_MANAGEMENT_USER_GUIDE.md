# Account Management - User Guide

## How to Manage Your Account

### Accessing Settings

1. Click on your profile or settings icon
2. Navigate to **Settings** page
3. Scroll down to **Account Management** section (red danger zone)

---

## Deactivate Account (Temporary)

### What Happens:
- ✅ Your profile is hidden from other users
- ✅ Your posts and comments remain visible
- ✅ You can reactivate anytime by logging in
- ✅ All your data is preserved

### Steps:

1. **Click "Deactivate Account" button**
   ```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 🚫 Deactivate Account           │
   │                                 │
   │ Temporarily disable your        │
   │ account. Reactivate anytime.    │
   │                                 │
   │ [Deactivate Account]            │
   └─────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

2. **Confirm in modal**
   ```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Deactivate Account?             │
   │                                 │
   │ Your account will be            │
   │ temporarily disabled.           │
   │                                 │
   │ [Cancel] [Deactivate]           │
   └─────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

3. **Account deactivated**
   - You'll be logged out
   - Redirected to login page
   - Can reactivate by logging in again

## Delete Account Permanently (Irreversible)

### ⚠️ WARNING:
- ❌ This CANNOT be undone
- ❌ All your data will be PERMANENTLY deleted
- ❌ Your posts and comments will be removed
- ❌ Your profile will be erased forever

### What Gets Deleted:
- All posts and comments
- All votes and awards
- Profile and account data
- Appointments and messages
- Community memberships
- Followers and following
- Everything!

### Steps:

1. **Review your account data**
   ```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
   │ ℹ️ Your Account Data:            │
   │                                 │
   │ 25 posts    150 comments        │
   │ 500 votes   5 communities       │
   │ 20 followers  15 following      │
   └─────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

2. **Click "Delete Account Permanently" button**
   ```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
   │ ⚠️ Delete Account Permanently    │
   │                                 │
   │ WARNING: This cannot be undone! │
   │                                 │
   │ All your data will be           │
   │ permanently deleted.            │
   │                                 │
   │ [Delete Account Permanently]    │
   └─────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

3. **Type confirmation text**
   ```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Delete Account Permanently?     │
   │                                 │
   │ ⚠️ This action cannot be undone! │
   │                                 │
   │ Type "DELETE MY ACCOUNT":       │
   │ ┌─────────────────────────────┐ │
   │ │ DELETE MY ACCOUNT           │ │
   │ └─────────────────────────────┘ │
   │                                 │
   │ [Cancel] [Delete Forever]       │
   └─────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

4. **Account deleted**
   - All data permanently removed
   - You'll be logged out
   - Redirected to homepage
   - Cannot be recovered

## Reactivating Your Account

If you deactivated your account:

1. Go to login page
2. Enter your username/email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. Your account will be automatically reactivated
5. All your data will be restored

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I recover a deleted account?
**No.** Once you delete your account permanently, it cannot be recovered. All data is permanently erased.

### What happens to my posts after deactivation?
Your posts remain visible to other users, but your profile is hidden.

### What happens to my posts after deletion?
All your posts are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

### How long does deletion take?
Deletion is immediate. Once confirmed, your account and all data are deleted within seconds.

### Can I cancel deletion?
Only if you haven't confirmed yet. Once you click "Delete Forever" and the process completes, it cannot be cancelled.

### Will my username be available again?
After permanent deletion, your username becomes available for others to use.

### What about my appointments?
- **Deactivate**: Appointments are preserved
- **Delete**: All appointments are cancelled and deleted

### What about my messages?
- **Deactivate**: Messages are preserved
- **Delete**: All messages are permanently deleted

### Can I download my data first?
Currently, there's no data export feature. Consider saving important content before deletion.
