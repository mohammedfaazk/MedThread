## Slide 4: Engagement Score Calculation
**Smart Algorithm**

```
Engagement Score = 
  (Posts × 10) + 
  (Comments × 5) + 
  (Likes × 2) + 
  (Shares × 8) + 
  (Time Spent ÷ 60) + 
  (Return Visits × 15)
```

Weighted by:
- Recency (recent activity counts more)
- Quality (helpful content scores higher)
- Consistency (regular users get bonus)

*Fair and comprehensive measurement*
