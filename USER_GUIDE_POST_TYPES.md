# User Guide: Creating Different Post Types

## 📝 Text Posts

**Best for**: Questions, discussions, stories, advice

**Steps**:
1. Click "Create Post" button
2. "Text" tab is selected by default
3. Enter your title
4. Write your content (optional)
5. Select a community
6. Add flair (optional)
7. Click "Post"

**Example**:
```
Title: "How to manage high blood pressure naturally?"
Content: "I was recently diagnosed with high blood pressure. 
My doctor recommended lifestyle changes before medication. 
What has worked for you?"
Community: m/cardiology
```

---

## 📷 Image Posts

**Best for**: Medical images, charts, infographics, before/after photos

**Steps**:
1. Click "Create Post"
2. Click "Image" tab
3. Enter your title
4. Click "Upload Images" or drag & drop
5. Select one or more images
6. Preview appears - remove any if needed
7. Add caption (optional)
8. Select community
9. Click "Post"

**Tips**:
- Max 10MB per image
- Supports: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP
- Can upload multiple images
- Add captions to explain images

**Example**:
```
Title: "My weight loss progress - 6 months"
Images: [before.jpg, after.jpg]
Caption: "Lost 30 pounds through diet and exercise!"
Community: m/general
```

---

## 🎥 Video Posts

**Best for**: Exercise demonstrations, medical procedures, educational content

**Steps**:
1. Click "Create Post"
2. Click "Image" tab (handles both images and videos)
3. Enter your title
4. Upload video file
5. Add caption (optional)
6. Select community
7. Click "Post"

**Tips**:
- Max 100MB per video
- Supports: MP4, WebM, QuickTime
- Add captions to describe video content

**Example**:
```
Title: "Proper form for knee exercises"
Video: knee_exercises.mp4
Caption: "Physical therapist demonstrates 5 exercises 
for knee pain relief"
Community: m/orthopedics
```

---

## 🔗 Link Posts

**Best for**: Sharing articles, research papers, news, resources

**Steps**:
1. Click "Create Post"
2. Click "Link" tab
3. Enter your title
4. Paste the URL
5. Add description (optional)
6. Select community
7. Click "Post"

**Tips**:
- URL must be valid (http:// or https://)
- Add description to explain why you're sharing
- Great for sharing medical research

**Example**:
```
Title: "New study on Mediterranean diet and heart health"
URL: https://example.com/study
Description: "Interesting findings on how diet affects 
cardiovascular health. Worth a read!"
Community: m/cardiology
```

---

## 📊 Poll Posts

**Best for**: Surveys, opinions, community feedback, quick questions

**Steps**:
1. Click "Create Post"
2. Click "Poll" tab
3. Enter your title
4. Fill in poll options (minimum 2, maximum 6)
5. Click "+ Add Option" for more choices
6. Select poll duration (1, 3, 7, or 14 days)
7. Select community
8. Click "Post"

**Tips**:
- Minimum 2 options required
- Maximum 6 options allowed
- Choose appropriate duration
- Make options clear and distinct

**Example**:
```
Title: "What time of day do you prefer to exercise?"
Options:
  - Early morning (5-8 AM)
  - Mid-morning (8-11 AM)
  - Afternoon (12-5 PM)
  - Evening (5-9 PM)
  - Night (9 PM+)
Duration: 7 days
Community: m/general
```

## 👁️ Managing Hidden Posts

### How to Hide a Post
1. Find any post in your feed
2. Click "Hide" button
3. Post disappears from feed
4. Post is saved to your hidden list

### How to View Hidden Posts
1. Look at sidebar
2. Find "Library" section
3. Click "Hidden"
4. See all your hidden posts

### How to Unhide a Post
1. Go to "Hidden" page
2. Find the post you want to unhide
3. Click "Unhide" button
4. Post reappears in your main feed

**Why hide posts?**
- Remove posts you're not interested in
- Clean up your feed
- Temporarily hide posts (can unhide later)
- Different from blocking (which is permanent)

## 🎨 Post Options

### Flair
- Optional tag for your post
- Options: Question, Discussion, Advice, Update, Emergency
- Helps categorize your post
- Makes it easier for others to find

### NSFW (Not Safe For Work)
- Check this for medical images that might be graphic
- Blurs content until user clicks to view
- Respectful to viewers

### Spoiler
- Hides content until clicked
- Use for sensitive medical information
- Protects privacy

## 📋 Best Practices

### Writing Good Titles
✅ **Good**: "Persistent headaches for 3 days - should I be concerned?"
❌ **Bad**: "Help!!!"

✅ **Good**: "New study: Mediterranean diet reduces heart disease risk by 30%"
❌ **Bad**: "Check this out"

### Choosing the Right Community
- **m/general** - General health questions
- **m/cardiology** - Heart and cardiovascular
- **m/neurology** - Brain and nervous system
- **m/pediatrics** - Children's health
- **m/mental-health** - Mental health and psychology
- **m/dermatology** - Skin conditions
- **m/orthopedics** - Bones, joints, muscles

### Adding Context
- Explain why you're posting
- Provide relevant background
- Be specific with questions
- Include relevant details (age, symptoms, duration)

---

## 🚫 What NOT to Post

- Personal medical records (protect privacy)
- Requests for diagnosis (see a doctor)
- Medication advice (consult healthcare provider)
- Graphic images without NSFW tag
- Spam or promotional content
- Misinformation

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I edit a post after publishing?**
A: Yes, for text posts. Image/video/link/poll editing coming soon.

**Q: How do I delete a post?**
A: Click the "..." menu on your post and select "Delete"

**Q: Can I upload multiple videos?**
A: Currently one video per post. Multiple images are supported.

**Q: What happens to hidden posts?**
A: They're saved in your "Hidden" page and can be unhidden anytime.

**Q: Can I change the poll duration after posting?**
A: No, poll duration is set when created.

**Q: What's the difference between Hide and Delete?**
A: Hide removes from your feed (reversible). Delete removes permanently.
