## ❓ ANTICIPATED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### Q: "How accurate is your disease prediction?"
**A**: "Our algorithms achieve 75-85% confidence scores. We're currently validating with medical institutions. The key is we're not diagnosing - we're predicting RISK, which helps with early intervention."

### Q: "How do you ensure doctor quality?"
**A**: "Three-layer verification: 1) Medical license verification, 2) Hospital affiliation check, 3) Ongoing performance tracking. Plus, we track success rates per condition, so quality is continuously monitored."

### Q: "What about patient privacy?"
**A**: "HIPAA-compliant architecture, end-to-end encryption, data anonymization in public forums, and comprehensive audit logging. Healthcare-grade security is non-negotiable."

### Q: "How do you compete with Practo?"
**A**: "Practo is reactive - they wait for symptoms. We're predictive - we detect diseases years early. Practo matches by specialty. We match by proven success rates. We're playing a different game."

### Q: "What's your revenue model?"
**A**: "Commission on consultations (15-20%), premium subscriptions ($9.99/month), API access for research institutions, and ethical sponsored content. Multiple revenue streams."

### Q: "How do you handle medical liability?"
**A**: "Clear disclaimers, liability waivers for high-risk activities, doctor verification, content moderation, and we're building partnerships with medical malpractice insurers."

### Q: "Can this scale globally?"
**A**: "Absolutely. Multi-language support (20+ languages), microservice architecture, cloud-native design, and we're building for horizontal scaling from day one."

### Q: "What's your user acquisition strategy?"
**A**: "Doctor-first approach - onboard top doctors, they bring patients. Community-driven growth through forums. Viral features like outbreak alerts. Partnerships with hospitals and clinics."

### Q: "How long to break even?"
**A**: "12-18 months with [X] funding. We need [Y] users and [Z] doctors to reach break-even. Conservative projections show profitability by month 18."

### Q: "What's your moat?"
**A**: "Data network effects - more users = better predictions = more users. Unique doctor performance dataset. Multi-modal AI algorithms. First-mover advantage in predictive healthcare."
