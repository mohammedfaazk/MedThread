# How to Use Community Features

## Quick Start Guide

### 1. Support Groups

**What are Support Groups?**
Connect with others facing similar health challenges in dedicated communities.

**How to Use:**

1. **Browse Groups**
   - Go to `/support-groups`
   - See all available groups with member counts
   - Filter by condition or search

2. **Join a Group**
   - Click on any group card
   - Click "Join Group" button
   - Start participating immediately

3. **Create a Group**
   - Click "Create Group" button
   - Fill in:
     - Group name (e.g., "Diabetes Support Circle")
     - Condition (e.g., "Diabetes")
     - Description
     - Privacy setting (public/private)
   - Click "Create Group"

4. **Post in Groups**
   - Open any group you've joined
   - Click "New Post"
   - Choose post type (Question, Discussion, Support, Update)
   - Write your post
   - Option to post anonymously in private groups

**Example Groups Available:**
- Diabetes Support Circle
- Heart Health Warriors
- Mental Wellness Hub
- Cancer Survivors Network
- PCOS Warriors

---

### 2. Health Challenges

**What are Health Challenges?**
Join fitness and wellness challenges to improve your health with community support.

**How to Use:**

1. **Browse Challenges**
   - Go to `/health-challenges`
   - See all active challenges
   - View difficulty, category, and participant count

2. **Join a Challenge**
   - Click on any challenge card
   - Read the details and requirements
   - Click "Join Challenge"
   - Note: HIGH-RISK challenges need doctor approval first

3. **Track Progress**
   - Go to "My Challenges" tab
   - Update your progress regularly
   - View your points and ranking
   - Check the leaderboard

4. **Create a Challenge (Doctors/Admins Only)**
   - Go to `/admin/health-challenges`
   - Fill in challenge details:
     - Title (e.g., "10,000 Steps Daily")
     - Description
     - Category (Fitness, Nutrition, Mental Health, etc.)
     - Difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
     - Risk Level (LOW or HIGH)
     - Duration (days)
     - Target metric and value
   - Click "Create Challenge"

**Risk Levels:**
- **LOW-RISK**: Auto-approved, anyone can join immediately
- **HIGH-RISK**: Requires doctor approval before patients can join

**Example Challenges Available:**
- 10,000 Steps Daily Challenge (Beginner, LOW-RISK)
- Sugar-Free September (Intermediate, LOW-RISK)
- Meditation Mastery (Beginner, LOW-RISK)
- Hydration Hero (Beginner, LOW-RISK)
- Strength Training Starter (Intermediate, HIGH-RISK)
- Sleep Schedule Reset (Intermediate, LOW-RISK)

### 3. Success Stories

**What are Success Stories?**
Share your health journey and inspire others with your recovery and wellness stories.

**How to Use:**

1. **Browse Stories**
   - Go to `/success-stories`
   - Read inspiring health journeys
   - Filter by condition
   - Like and comment on stories

2. **Share Your Story**
   - Click "Share Your Story" button
   - Fill in:
     - Title (e.g., "How I Beat Diabetes in 6 Months")
     - Condition (select from dropdown)
     - Your story (describe your journey)
   - Click "Share Story"
   - Story is immediately visible (auto-approved in development)

3. **Engage with Stories**
   - Click ❤️ to like a story
   - Click 💬 to read and add comments
   - Share encouragement and support

**Example Stories Available:**
- How I Reversed My Pre-Diabetes in 6 Months
- My Journey from Anxiety to Peace
- Beating PCOS: My 2-Year Transformation
- Heart Attack Survivor: One Year Later
- Conquering Depression: My Story
- Living Well with Rheumatoid Arthritis
- Thyroid Balance: My Healing Journey
- Asthma Control: From ER Visits to Active Life

## Tips for Best Experience

### Support Groups
- ✅ Be respectful and supportive
- ✅ Share experiences, not medical advice
- ✅ Use anonymous posting for sensitive topics
- ✅ Follow group rules

### Health Challenges
- ✅ Choose challenges matching your fitness level
- ✅ Update progress regularly
- ✅ Consult your doctor before HIGH-RISK challenges
- ✅ Stay motivated by checking the leaderboard

### Success Stories
- ✅ Be honest and authentic
- ✅ Focus on your journey and what helped
- ✅ Avoid sharing sensitive medical details
- ✅ Inspire others with hope and positivity

## Troubleshooting

**Can't join a challenge?**
- Check if it's HIGH-RISK and awaiting doctor approval
- Ensure you're logged in
- Try refreshing the page

**Group not showing posts?**
- Make sure you've joined the group
- Check if it's a private group requiring approval

**Story not appearing?**
- Stories are auto-approved in development
- Check the "All Stories" filter
- Refresh the page

---

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you're logged in
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Contact support if problems persist
